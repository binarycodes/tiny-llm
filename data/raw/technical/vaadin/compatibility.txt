List of Supported Technologies

Lists the minimum required version of compatible technologies for Vaadin 25.

This page shows the minimum required versions of supported technologies for Vaadin 25. See the release notes for more details.

Technology | Version
Java | 21 or later
Spring Boot | 4.1 or later
Node.js | 24 or later
NPM | 11.3 or later
PNPM | 10.16 or later
Maven | 3.8 or later
Gradle | 8.14 or later
Servlet | 6.1 or later
Application Servers | - Apache Tomcat 11 - Open Liberty 23 - RedHat JBoss Enterprise Application Platform (EAP) 8.1 - WildFly 36 - Jetty 12 - Payara Server 6 - Payara Micro 6
Quarkus | 3.27 or later
Portlet | Not supported yet
OSGi | Not supported yet
Karaf | Not supported yet
